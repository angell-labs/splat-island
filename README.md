# Splat Island

A kid-friendly cartoon paint-blaster battle game for iPad and iPhone Safari (also works on desktop browsers).

- Third-person view, two-thumb touch controls: joystick, big drag-to-aim FIRE button, auto-fire, aim assist, lock-on and a look-speed slider
- Build walls and ramps; shoot trees, crates, barrels and fences for wood
- 7 paint and foam weapons with rarity colors: Splat Blaster, Foam Shotgun, Paint Rifle, Splat SMG, Bubble Sniper, Balloon Launcher, Glitter Rocket. Find them in chests and on the floor, carry up to 5
- Hero skins: 12 original superheroes (Captain Splash, Thunder Tess, Nova Knight, Shadow Fox, Blaze Runner, Frost Byte, Jungle Jax, Robo Rex, Star Sprinkle, Turbo Tortoise, Comet Kid, Mighty Mango), each with 3 styles. All free, nothing to buy
- Locker: pick a hero, or make a custom look with skin tone, hair, outfit colors, hats, back bling and glider trail
- Emotes: dance, wave and flex (B key or the 😃 button)
- Climate zones: snowy Frosty Peak with a ski lodge, Windmill Hills, Jungle Lagoon with a waterfall, Cactus Canyon desert, sandy beaches, meadows and pine woods
- Weather: drifting clouds, sun, rain showers with splashes, rainbows, mountain fog and snow
- Wood plank walls and ramps that assemble piece by piece and crack before they break
- Shrinking storm circle and silly bots, Easy and Normal difficulty
- On a computer: click to aim with the mouse, WASD/arrows move, Space or left-click fire, Shift jump, E build, Q wall/ramp, 1–5 or wheel switch weapons, L or right-click lock-on, B emote, Esc frees the mouse
- Solo, or 2 to 4 iPads (or computers) with a 4-letter room code (one iPad hosts)

Play: https://angell-labs.github.io/splat-island/

All original characters and art. Knocked-out players poof into confetti.
