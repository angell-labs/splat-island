📡 Play on 2–4 iPads
One iPad taps Host and gets a 4-letter code. The other iPads type the code and tap Join. Every iPad needs internet.
How to play: left thumb moves, drag on the right side to look around, hold FIRE to splat (you can drag the Fire button to aim too). BUILD drops a wall or ramp in front of you (tap the small button to switch). Grab blasters 🟠🫧, health ❤️ and bricks 🧱. Stay out of the purple storm! Knocked-out players go POOF into confetti. Keyboard: WASD move, mouse drag look, Space jump, F fire, Q build, R wall/ramp, E switch blaster.

🔄 Turn your iPad sideways to play!
