📡 Play on 2–4 iPads
One iPad taps Host and gets a 4-letter code. The other iPads type the code and tap Join. Every iPad needs internet.
How to play: left thumb moves, drag on the right side to look around, hold the big FIRE button and slide your thumb on it to aim while you shoot. Auto-fire shoots by itself when the circle turns red, and Aim assist gently pulls the circle onto enemies. Tap 🎯 LOCK to stick to the nearest enemy. BUILD drops a wall or ramp in front of you (tap the small button to switch). Grab blasters 🟠🫧, health ❤️ and bricks 🧱. Stay out of the purple storm! Knocked-out players go POOF into confetti. On a computer: click the game to aim with the mouse, WASD or arrows move, Space or left-click fires (hold for more), Shift jumps, E builds, Q switches wall/ramp, 1/2/3 or the mouse wheel switch blasters, L or right-click locks on, Esc frees the mouse.
